Common questions.

How long does delivery take?

Orders are processed within 2 to 5 working days. Standard delivery then takes about 7 to 20 working days, international delivery about 10 to 25. You get a shipping confirmation with tracking as soon as your order leaves.

How much is shipping?

The exact fee is shown at checkout, before you pay.

Can I return my order?

Unused items in their original packaging can be returned within 30 days of delivery. Email support@glampearl.store to start a return. For hygiene reasons, opened personal-care and beauty products cannot be returned.

Which payment methods do you accept?

All major cards and the express wallets offered at the secure Shopify checkout, including Apple Pay. Prices are shown in your local currency.

Is gel polish hard to use at home?

No. Prepare your nails, paint a thin layer, cure it under the LED lamp, and repeat for each layer. The builder gel, a shade and the USB lamp are all you need to start.
